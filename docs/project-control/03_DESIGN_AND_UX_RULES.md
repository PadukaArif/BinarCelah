# BINARCELAH — DESIGN & UX RULES

## Authority
The supplied four Figma PDF exports are the approved visual reference for the First Draft. Match their layout, hierarchy, spacing, color, typography, and interaction intent. Do not invent a new visual direction.

## Design character
Editorial investigative platform + public-service usability + structured evidence tracing.

Core principle: **Narrative first. Evidence always accessible. Uncertainty never hidden.**

## Existing visual foundation
- Typeface: Inter.
- Navy Blue: `#112250`
- Sapphire: `#3B507D`
- Warm Taupe: `#BEB7A7`
- Champagne: `#E7E2CE`
- Ivory: `#F5F4F0`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Use the palette consistently and validate contrast for actual text/control combinations. Do not use color alone to communicate status.

## UX rules
- Explain the product immediately in plain Indonesian.
- Keep navigation predictable and labels consistent.
- Prioritize the main content over decorative cards and metrics.
- Put evidence close to the claim it supports.
- Let users inspect source details and limitations.
- Explain why records are connected and what the connection does not establish.
- Use clear loading, empty, error, unavailable, and review-pending states.
- Ensure keyboard focus is visible and controls have accessible names.
- Responsive behavior must preserve reading order and functionality on mobile.

## Interaction integrity
Every visible control must have a real outcome:
- Navigation links navigate.
- Search filters actual available records or is omitted.
- Expand/collapse controls reveal real content.
- Source links open the cited source.
- Forms validate and clearly communicate whether submission is actually stored.
Do not show success messages for actions that are not connected to a working process.

## Content integrity in the supplied screens
Metrics, counts, hashes, verification labels, PPID identifiers, and case claims in the PDFs are not independently verified by their appearance in a mockup. Do not implement them as factual data without source validation.
